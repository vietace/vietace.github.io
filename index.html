Everything on your LAN

Built for people who keep their own media

Point PhimCa at a share and it does the rest — discover, browse, cast, or play. No transcoding servers, no subscriptions.

Cast to any DLNA screen

Send video and music to TVs, laser projectors and audio receivers over standard DLNA — Samsung, LG, Hisense, OPPO and more.

Browse SMB & NAS

Connect to SMB/CIFS shares on your NAS or PC. Fast auto-discovery finds hosts and shares on your network in seconds.

Cloud drives too

Play and cast straight from Google Drive, OneDrive and Dropbox — right alongside your NAS and PC, in the same queue.

Bit-perfect audio

Streams the original samples with no resampling, downmixing or volume scaling — your renderer's DAC receives every bit intact.

Hi-res & DSD

FLAC, ALAC, WavPack, AIFF, DSF/DSD and more — including high sample-rate sources, handled with format-accurate delivery.

Subtitles that work

Load .srt, .vtt, .ass and .ssa alongside your video and switch tracks mid-playback while casting.

Playlists & gapless

Build a queue, shuffle, repeat, and let gapless casting roll to the next track automatically — even in the background.
